ARTICUNO · ZAPDOS · MOLTRES
LEGENDARY BIRDS GUIDE

All three legendary birds are available in Fire Red and Leaf Green before or shortly after the Elite Four. Step-by-step locations and catching walkthroughs for each.

#144 ARTICUNO

The Freeze Pokémon — found deep in the Seafoam Islands. Requires solving a boulder puzzle to reach. Ice/Flying type with high Special Defense.

IceFlying
Lv. 50
Sp.DEF 100
Sp.ATK 95
How to catch Articuno — Seafoam Islands
1

Get Surf & Strength Before You Go

Seafoam Islands require both Surf (HM03 — Safari Zone) and Strength (HM04 — Fuchsia City warden). Without both HMs, you can't reach Articuno. Make sure your party has Pokémon that can use both.

2

Reach the Seafoam Islands via Route 20

Surf west from Cinnabar Island (or east from Fuchsia City) along Route 20. You'll reach two islands in the middle of the sea — this is the Seafoam Islands cave entrance. Enter from either island's entrance.

3

Solve the Boulder Puzzle (B3F & B4F)

The cave has a strong underwater current that prevents you from Surfing to Articuno. To slow the current, you need to push four boulders into holes using Strength:

🪨 Puzzle guide: On B3F, push the boulders you find into the holes in the floor. Two boulders on B3F, two on B4F. Each boulder dropped into a hole reduces the current. After all four are in place, the current stops and you can Surf to Articuno's chamber on B4F.
4

Save & Battle Articuno on B4F

Navigate to the lowest floor (B4F) and Surf north through the now-still water. Articuno is at the far end of the chamber. Save your game, then step up to trigger the battle. It's level 50 with Powder Snow, Mist, Ice Beam, and Agility.

💡 Weakness: Articuno is 4× weak to Rock-type moves, and weak to Fire, Electric, and Steel. Use a Rock-type to lower its HP quickly, then switch to False Swipe to land on 1 HP. Apply Sleep, then throw Ultra Balls.
⚠️

Don't use your Master Ball here! Articuno is challenging but very catchable with 20 Ultra Balls + sleep. Save your Master Ball for the roaming legendary dogs, which are far harder to catch.

#145 ZAPDOS

The Electric Pokémon — the easiest of the three birds to reach. No puzzle required, just Surf to the Power Plant on Route 10.

ElectricFlying
Lv. 50
Sp.ATK 125
SPD 100
How to catch Zapdos — Power Plant
1

Get Surf First

You need Surf (HM03 from the Safari Zone) to access the Power Plant. Without it, you can't cross the water to the building's entrance.

2

Head to Route 10 North of Lavender Town

Route 10 runs south from the Rock Tunnel entrance. Use Surf on the river that flows through Route 10 — ride the current east toward the Power Plant building, a large industrial structure on the water's edge.

🗺️ Easy route: From Lavender Town, go north then west to find the Rock Tunnel entrance. The river that flows past it is where you Surf to reach the Power Plant.
3

Navigate Through the Power Plant

The Power Plant is a single-floor building filled with wild Electric Pokémon (Magnemite, Voltorb, Electabuzz) and item balls — some of which are disguised Voltorbs. Navigate to the far end of the building where Zapdos waits.

⚡ Pokémon tip: Bring a Ground-type Pokémon to deal with all the wild Electric encounters here. Dugtrio or Nidoking work great.
4

Save & Battle Zapdos

Zapdos is at the dead end of the Power Plant. Save before approaching it. At level 50, it uses Thunder Wave, ThunderShock, DrillPeck, and Thunder. Its Electric moves can paralyze you, so bring Paralyz Heals.

💡 Weakness: Zapdos is weak to Ice and Rock. Use Rock Slide or Ancient Power to chip it down, then False Swipe to 1 HP. Sleep it and throw Ultra Balls. Zapdos has higher catch difficulty than Articuno, so bring 25+ Ultra Balls.

#146 MOLTRES

The Flame Pokémon — moved from Victory Road to Mt. Ember on One Island in FRLG. Requires progressing through the Sevii Islands questline to access.

FireFlying
Lv. 50
Sp.ATK 125
SPD 90
🔥

FRLG Change: In the original Red/Blue, Moltres was in Victory Road. In Fire Red and Leaf Green, it was relocated to Mt. Ember on One Island in the Sevii Islands. Don't look for it at Victory Road — it's not there.

How to catch Moltres — Mt. Ember, One Island
1

Reach One Island via the Sevii Islands Ferry

After obtaining the first two Badges and talking to Bill in Cerulean City, you'll be invited to the Sevii Islands. The ferry from Vermilion takes you to One Island (also called Knot Island). This is available early in the game.

2

Complete the One Island Questline (Boon Island)

On your first visit to One Island, Bill's friend Celio asks you to find the Ruby stone on Mt. Ember. Go north through One Island town to reach Mt. Ember's base. You'll need to defeat two Team Rocket grunts who are guarding a hidden cave entrance near the summit.

📜 Team Rocket cave: Near the top of Mt. Ember's exterior, two Rocket grunts are standing next to a cave entrance. Defeat them and enter — the Ruby is inside. Deliver it to Celio to progress the Sevii Islands story.
3

Return to Mt. Ember — Enter the Summit Path

After completing the initial questline and having Strength, return to Mt. Ember. The path to the summit requires using Strength to push boulders. Work your way up through the rocky interior of the mountain toward the peak.

4

Save & Battle Moltres at the Summit

Moltres waits at the very top of Mt. Ember. Save before engaging. At level 50, it uses FireSpin, Agility, Ember, and Leer. Fire Spin can trap you for several turns — have a Water or Rock type ready to take hits.

💡 Weakness: Moltres is 4× weak to Rock-type moves and also weak to Water and Electric. A Kabutops with Rock Slide or Lapras with Surf can chip it down quickly. Then False Swipe and sleep before throwing Ultra Balls.
